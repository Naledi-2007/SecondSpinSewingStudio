WHO WE ARE

About Second Spin

Second Spin Sewing Studio is a non-profit organisation focused on repairing, reusing and upcycling clothing.

We want to show our community that clothing does not always need to be replaced when it becomes damaged or unwanted. With sewing and creativity, garments can be given another chance.

OUR PURPOSE

Mission & Vision

01

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce clothing waste through repairs, alterations, upcycling and community sewing initiatives.

We also aim to share useful sewing skills that encourage people to reuse the clothing they already own.

02

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a community where people think differently about clothing waste and see value in repairing and reusing garments.

We hope to encourage more sustainable habits while supporting members of our community.

WHY IT MATTERS

Sustainable Fashion

Sustainable fashion encourages people to think about how clothing is used, cared for and eventually discarded.

Repair

Fixing damaged garments can extend the amount of time that they can be worn.

Reuse

Clothing can be donated, restyled or reused instead of being immediately discarded.

Upcycle

Creativity can turn old garments into something different and useful.
